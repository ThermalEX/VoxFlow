# VoxFlow Rewrite v5 — withdrawn

**Status: discontinued. No model weights are available in this directory.**

V5 was trained and evaluated on 2026-10-18, but was withdrawn before its public release. Although it improved correction of some fixed expressions, the overall experience was worse than v4: suggestions leaned more heavily toward tone changes, and style rewriting became less reliable. The v5 model files were removed. This directory documents the skipped version number; it is not a downloadable model release.

For a usable model, choose [v6](https://huggingface.co/ThermalEX/voxflow-rewrite/tree/main/v6) (current recommendation) or [v4](https://huggingface.co/ThermalEX/voxflow-rewrite/tree/main/v4). The [VoxFlow research log](https://github.com/ThermalEX/VoxFlow/blob/main/docs/research-log.md) retains the evaluation and explains the decision.

---

**状态：已弃用。本目录没有可下载的模型权重。**

V5 于 2026 年 10 月 18 日完成训练和评测，但在公开发布前被放弃。它改进了部分名句、成语纠错，实际体验却不如 V4：改写建议更偏向语气调整，风格改写的可靠性也下降。V5 模型文件随后被删除。本目录仅用于解释为何版本号从 V4 跳到 V6，**不代表 V5 仍可安装或使用**。

需要可用模型时，请选择 [V6](https://huggingface.co/ThermalEX/voxflow-rewrite/tree/main/v6)（当前推荐）或 [V4](https://huggingface.co/ThermalEX/voxflow-rewrite/tree/main/v4)。详细对比和弃用原因见 [VoxFlow 研究记录](https://github.com/ThermalEX/VoxFlow/blob/main/docs/research-log.md)。
